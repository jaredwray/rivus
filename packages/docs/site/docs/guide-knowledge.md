# Training Rivus with FAQs

Rivus answers customers from **your** content — the services, prices, and
FAQ answers on your account. Nothing is pooled from other businesses. This
guide covers how to keep that knowledge sharp.

## Where knowledge lives

In the app, open **Knowledge**. Each entry is a question and the answer you
want customers to get, in your voice. Your onboarding specialist writes the
first batch during setup; from then on it's yours to grow.

## Writing answers that work

- **Answer like you would on the phone.** Short, direct, friendly. The
  answer is shown or spoken nearly verbatim, so write it customer-ready.
- **One question per entry.** "Do you service my area?" and "What do you
  charge for a service call?" are two entries, not one.
- **Include the numbers you're happy to share.** Service-call fees, hours,
  and service areas resolve most conversations instantly. Skip anything you
  want a human to quote personally.
- **Mind duplicates.** Rivus warns you when a new entry looks like an
  existing one, so conflicting answers don't creep in.

## What happens without an answer

Rivus sticks to what it knows: booking appointments, telling a customer what
they already have on your calendar, and the answers you've given it. Keep an
eye on your inbox for the conversations that need a human — they're your
knowledge to-do list, and each one you turn into an FAQ makes the next
customer's answer instant.

## Keeping it current

Prices change; seasons change. When you update an entry, Rivus uses the new
answer immediately — there's nothing to retrain or redeploy.
