# Connecting your channels

Rivus answers on four channels — phone calls, SMS, email, and WhatsApp —
and lands every conversation in one inbox. This guide covers how each is
connected and the messaging rules that keep your texting compliant.

## Phone, SMS, and WhatsApp

Phone-based channels are enabled per channel from your account settings.
Your onboarding specialist handles this with you during setup, including
connecting the business number your customers already know. Each channel
can be turned on or off independently, and your account keeps its number
identity — disabling a channel doesn't discard it.

## Email

Your account gets its own agent address on `riv.us`, and Rivus replies in
the same thread your customer started — real email threading, not a ticket
number. Ask your specialist about routing your existing business address.

## The texting rules (worth two minutes)

Texting customers comes with carrier and legal rules, and Rivus is built
around them:

- **Consent first.** Rivus messages people who contacted your business
  first, or who opted in on your booking form — and consent belongs to your
  business alone; it is never transferred.
- **STOP always works.** Opt-outs are honored automatically, and HELP gets
  help. You may not message someone who has opted out.
- **Prohibited content is blocked territory.** Carriers ban certain
  categories outright (the "SHAFT" list and more) — see the
  [Acceptable Use Policy](https://rivus.ai/acceptable-use).

The full program details live in the site's
[SMS terms](https://rivus.ai/sms-terms) and
[opt-in description](https://rivus.ai/sms-opt-in).

## One inbox, whatever the channel

However a customer reaches you, the conversation lands in the same inbox
with the same history, and your team can step into any thread at any time.
